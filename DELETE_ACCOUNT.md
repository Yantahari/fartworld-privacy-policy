# How to Delete Your FartWorld Account

The App does not yet have a delete button. To delete your FartWorld account and its data, send an email to:

**fartworld.app@gmail.com**

Subject: "Delete my FartWorld account"

## If you registered with an email address

**Send the request from the email address of your account.** A request that only mentions an address, sent from a different one, cannot be accepted: it is how we make sure nobody can delete someone else's account.

## If you never registered (anonymous account)

Your account has no email, so we have to confirm it is yours another way:

1. Write to us and tell us your **username** in the App.
2. We reply and ask you to tell us when you have a few minutes.
3. We then send you a short instruction: **to save two new recordings, in order, of two lengths we give you** (for example "about 3 seconds, then about 9"), within the next 30 minutes. Only the person holding the account, and reading our email, can do that.
4. Once we see them, we delete the account, including those two recordings.

To protect you from someone else trying this on your account, we accept one such check per account every 30 days.

**If you no longer have access to the account** (you logged out, reset your profile, uninstalled the App or changed phone), tell us your username, roughly when you recorded, and your phone model. If we cannot tell with reasonable certainty that the account is yours, we will tell you so and explain why, and we will not delete it: the alternative would let anyone delete anyone else's recordings.

## What Will Be Deleted

- Your user profile (username, avatar, league/category, email if any) and your sign-in account
- Your recordings: audio files, scores and their place on the leaderboards
- Your team membership
- Your achievements and statistics
- Your push notification token

## What May Remain

- Points your recordings added to a team's total score may be kept, without any link to you.
- Likes you gave to other people's recordings may remain as a count.
- Purchase records may be kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- Backups: up to 7 days for the database and file storage, up to 14 days for our analytics copy.
- Analytics events (only if you had accepted analytics): we delete the ones tied to your user ID from our own analytics copy. Whatever Google Analytics still holds is no longer tied to any activity and expires within at most 14 months.

## Processing Time

Within **30 days** of receiving your request. We confirm by email when it is done.
